# IT Pathway Diagnostic

**Try it:** https://amymcmullin.github.io/IT-Pathway-Diagnostic/

A browser-based career assessment for students exploring information technology. It measures two things separately, **what students are drawn to** and **how they already reason**, across ten IT areas:

Support & hardware · Programming · Networking · Security · Database design · Data analytics · Cloud · Virtualization · Artificial intelligence · Project management

The results rank each student's strongest paths and list the industry certifications that lead into them. The certifications come from Florida's CAPE (Career and Professional Education) funding lists, sorted by difficulty and marked by eligibility for adult/postsecondary and dual-enrollment students.

Built by [Amy McMullin](https://amymcmullin.github.io) for the Computer Systems & Information Technology program at Immokalee Technical College (iTECH), Collier County Public Schools.

## How it works

| Part | Items | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Interest | 40 | Statements about the kind of work a student would choose. There are no right answers. |
| 2 — Reasoning | 80 (full) or 40 (short) | Short problems, one correct answer each, that need no prior IT knowledge. |

* Takes about 25–35 minutes. The short form of Part 2 is for when class time is tight.
* **Private by design.** Nothing is saved or sent anywhere, and answers stay in the student's browser tab. Students print or save their results at the end.
* A printable paper version is built in for classrooms without devices.

## Using it in your classroom

Share the link above. There's nothing to install and no accounts to create, and it works on any device with a modern browser.

To run your own copy, download `index.html` and open it in a browser, or fork this repository and turn on GitHub Pages.

## Updating the certification lists

The CAPE funding lists change each year, and the certification data is embedded in `index.html`. Update it there when the Florida DOE publishes new lists.
